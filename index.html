The series promise

The monster is never only the thing in the dark.

It is also the road, house, custom, title, ritual, or institution that learned to protect the wrong.

The High Pass Chronicles combines old-world atmosphere with strict supernatural laws, public proof, moral consequence, and hard-won release. Every novel stands alone. Readers may begin anywhere.

“The deepest terror is the custom that protected the wrong until someone made the truth visible.”

The laws of the High Pass

Atmosphere is never decoration.

⌁

The land remembers

Roads, bridges, houses, and mountain passes retain what people would rather forget.

≡

Objects carry proof

Ledgers, lamps, nails, bells, stones, doors, and masks turn hidden wrongdoing into evidence.

✦

Mercy must be proven

Private goodness is not enough. Repair and release must become visible in public practice.

◇

Power always costs

Every supernatural rule has a limit, and every choice leaves something behind.

About the author

R. Keith Parker

Old-world Gothic fantasy shaped by history, moral consequence, haunted places, and the stubborn courage of ordinary people.

A seventh-grade world history teacher from the hills of Tennessee, R. Keith Parker brings the pressure of law, faith, labor, hunger, architecture, and public memory into dark fantasy built for both atmosphere and consequence.

His stories favor dangerous roads, strict supernatural laws, haunted records, hard-won witness, and characters who survive through courage, discipline, and proof rather than easy rescue.
