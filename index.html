About the Author

R. Keith Parker

Old-world Gothic fantasy shaped by history, moral consequence, haunted places, and the stubborn courage of ordinary people.

A seventh-grade world history teacher from the hills of Tennessee, R. Keith Parker brings the pressure of law, faith, labor, hunger, architecture, and public memory into dark fantasy built for both atmosphere and consequence.

His stories favor dangerous roads, strict supernatural laws, haunted records, hard-won witness, and characters who survive through courage, discipline, and proof rather than easy rescue.

Reader Guide

Before you enter the pass

Do I need to read the books in order?

No. Each High Pass novel is designed to stand alone, with its own central wrong, supernatural system, setting, cast, and resolution. Reading in production order lets you watch the series’ craft and world expand, but it is not required.

What kind of fantasy is this?

Old-world Gothic fantasy with folk-horror pressure, strict supernatural laws, historical texture, moral consequence, and mystery built through objects, customs, and public proof.

Is the series mainly horror?

The books contain dread, haunted places, dangerous customs, bodily risk, and dark supernatural imagery, but the central movement is toward witness, resistance, release, and difficult forms of justice.

Which book has the clearest haunted-house premise?

The House That Would Not Release offers a strong custody-Gothic entry point, while The House That Kept the Last Lamp centers deathwatch, community selection, and false mercy.

Where can I buy the books?

Each catalog card links directly to its Kindle edition on Amazon. Paperback and hardcover editions are also available for the listed titles.
